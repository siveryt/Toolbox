LAN Scanner

See every device in your Wi-Fi network, with name and IP address.

Which IP address did the printer get? Is the new smart plug online? Is someone else using your Wi-Fi? The LAN Scanner in Toolbox searches your network and lists every device it finds.

What it can do

  • All devices in your network: computers, phones, printers, TVs, smart home devices – everything that answers in your Wi-Fi.
  • Names and IP addresses: each device shows up with its name and its IP address in your network.
  • Copy an address: copy a device's IP address to use it in another app, for example to open a router or printer page.
  • Your own address: see the IP address of your iPhone in the network.
  • Stays in your network: the scan only talks to the devices in your Wi-Fi, nothing is sent to the internet.

How to scan your network

  1. Connect your iPhone to the Wi-Fi network you want to scan.
  2. Open Toolbox and tap LAN Scanner.
  3. Allow access to the local network when asked.
  4. Wait a moment while Toolbox finds the devices, then copy the address of the one you need.
