Dice Roller

Roll one die or several, with 4 to 20 sides, on your iPhone or Apple Watch.

Forgot the dice for your board game, or need a D20 for your next role-playing session? The dice in Toolbox are always in your pocket. Shake your iPhone or tap to roll, and hold a die to keep it for the next throw.

What it can do

  • Every common die: D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 and D20, for board games, dice games and tabletop role-playing games.
  • Several dice at once: pick how many dice you need and roll them all together.
  • Shake or tap to roll: shake your iPhone like a dice cup, or tap the button.
  • Keep dice: hold a die to lock it. Locked dice keep their number when you roll again, for games where you set some dice aside and throw the rest.
  • On your wrist: the Apple Watch app rolls a six-sided die too.

How to roll

  1. Open Toolbox and tap Dice.
  2. Choose the number of sides and how many dice you want.
  3. Shake your iPhone or tap to roll.
  4. Hold a die to keep it, then roll the others again.
