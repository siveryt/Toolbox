Privacy Policy for Toolbox

Effective Date: August 9, 2022

Christian Nagel built the Toolbox app as an open-source application. This Service is provided by Christian Nagel at no cost and is intended for use as is. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Toolbox app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Toolbox is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: Toolbox does not request, collect, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location).
  • No Usage Data: We do not track your activity within the app.
  • No Device Data: Toolbox does not collect information about your device, including its operating system, IP address, or hardware specifications.

2. Log Data

Toolbox does not actively collect any log data. However, in the event of a crash or error, information about the issue may be stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or any third-party service.

3. Cookies

Toolbox does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

4. Third-Party Services

Toolbox does not integrate with or rely on any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks that could collect or share data about you.

5. Security

Although Toolbox does not collect or process data, we strive to ensure the app is secure and free of vulnerabilities. However, please note that no software can guarantee 100% security.

6. Links to External Sites

Toolbox does not include links to external sites. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and provide clear notice within the app.

7. Children’s Privacy

Toolbox does not knowingly collect or process any data, including from children under the age of 16. Parents or guardians are encouraged to review apps their children use.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the app or applicable laws. Any updates will be posted within the app, and the effective date will be revised accordingly.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact me at:

Email: toolbox@sivery.de

10. Disclaimer

This privacy policy is based on our understanding of your expectations and relevant legal requirements. It was initially created using online privacy policy generators and has been further tailored to ensure clarity and transparency regarding our data practices.
