Toolbox bundles the small tools you need every now and then into one tidy app – so you don't need a separate app for every little task.

TOOLS
• Dice and Random Letter – shake to roll, hold a die to keep it
• Random Number, Counter and Live Clock
• Date Difference – count the days between two dates
• Roman Numbers – convert both ways
• QR-Code Generator – for links, text, Wi-Fi and email
• Barcode Scanner – with a history of your scanned codes
• Color Picker – with HEX and RGB codes
• BMI calculator
• Network tools: IP-Checker, Domain Resolver, Ping, LAN Scanner and Wake On Lan
• Location: Coordinates (decimal, DMS and Maidenhead), Speed and Distance
• Sound: Metronome and Decibel Meter
• Pulsing Flashlight, Scrolling Text and Font Installer

MAKE IT YOURS
Hide the tools you don't use, move your favourites to the top and pick one of ten app icons, including a Pride icon.

ALSO ON APPLE WATCH
Dice, Counter and Random Number are on your wrist, and a widget rolls the dice right on your Home Screen.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Toolbox keeps your data on your device.

OPEN SOURCE
Toolbox is open source. Help translate it or add your own tool: toolbox.sivery.de/contribution.html
